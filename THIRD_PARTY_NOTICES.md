## Chromium dino game artwork (T-Rex, cacti, pterodactyl silhouettes)

The sprite silhouettes in `src/ui/dino.rs` were reproduced block by block
from the pixel art of Chromium's offline dino game
(`components/neterror/resources/dino_game/`, `offline_sprite`), and the
gameplay constants were re-implemented from the same source without copying
code. See `docs/dino.md` for the full comparison.
